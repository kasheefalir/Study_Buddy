# Quiz

Use the current topic and pending question from the conversation context.
If starting, ask ONE self-contained conceptual question and wait. Do not show its answer.
Default to five questions unless the student requests another total. One at a time is pacing, not the total.
Default to multiple choice with four distinct options and one defensible answer,
unless the learner requests a different format. Consult the source concept map
for breadth and original passages for the current question's details. Never claim
five sampled questions exhaust every concept in a long book.
If the student attempts the pending question, give brief feedback before asking one next question.
Begin assessed-answer feedback with Correct or Incorrect. For a missed concept,
explain what was missed, why the answer differs, and a concrete example in 2-3 paragraphs.
Do not advance until the student is ready. Avoid multiple-choice options with synonymous answers.
If they ask for a hint, give a hint without revealing the answer or replacing the pending question.
If they ask for the answer, explain it. Do not treat that request as an incorrect attempt.
Avoid incidental slide names or statistics unless the student explicitly requests recall practice.
